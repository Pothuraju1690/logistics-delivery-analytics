# Logistics Delivery Performance & Cost Analytics

## Executive Summary

The objective of this project is to evaluate logistics operations, delivery performance, transportation cost, warehouse efficiency and partner performance.

---

## Business Problem

Management lacks visibility into:

- Delivery delays
- Route efficiency
- Warehouse performance
- Cost management
- Partner performance

---

## Data Summary

Dataset contains:

- Orders
- Warehouses
- Delivery partners
- Costs
- Delivery dates
- Customer ratings

---

## Data Quality

Issues identified:

- Missing values
- Duplicate records
- Invalid dates
- Invalid numeric values

---

## Cleaning Performed

- Missing value handling
- Duplicate removal
- Date conversion
- Numeric validation
- Business rule validation
- Derived column creation

---

## KPI Summary

- Total Orders
- Delivered Orders
- Delayed Orders
- On Time %
- Average Delivery Days
- Total Logistics Cost

---

## Key Findings

- Some routes show higher delays
- Certain warehouses require investigation
- Transportation cost differs by mode
- Delivery partners perform differently

---

## Business Recommendations

1. Review high-delay routes
2. Optimize warehouse operations
3. Negotiate transportation costs
4. Monitor partner SLAs

---

## Conclusion

The project provides data-driven visibility into logistics performance and operational costs.
